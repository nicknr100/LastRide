01 / OUR STORY

Small moments. Real problems. Thoughtful engineering.

LastRide began with a question: What if getting home on time was one less thing to think about? We set out to turn the uncertainty of the last train into a calm, dependable experience.

We're computer science students at Waseda University's School of Fundamental Science and Engineering, expecting to graduate in Fall 2028. We engineer the full experience — from reliable systems and infrastructure to simple, intuitive interfaces — because good software has to work in the real world.
