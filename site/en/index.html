FAQ

Common questions

Does it work outside Tokyo?

Yes. JR, private railways and subways across Japan come from Apple Maps; Tokyo Metro, Toei and a few others use official timetable data.

What’s free?

Your leave time, reminders and alarms, and the plan B if you miss it are all free. Widgets, the lock-screen countdown and creating groups are Premium.

Do I need an account?

No. Your destinations and settings stay on your iPhone, and no movement history is kept.

How is my location used?

Only to find the nearest station and walking time. It’s used while the app is closed only if you choose “When closed” for night tracking.

Is the app in English?

Yes. LastRide works in English and Japanese — switch any time in Settings.

Is there an Android version?

It’s on the way. A download link will appear on this page when it’s out.
